# 🎓 CampusHub – Smart College Assistant

## 📌 About the Project

CampusHub is a smart college assistance web application designed to help students easily access important college-related information from one platform.

Students often face problems during admission, scholarship applications, examinations, and other college processes. They may not know:

* Where to submit a form
* Which documents are required
* Where the scholarship office is located
* Where to find examination information
* How to locate the library, department, HOD office, or other college offices
* Whom to contact for a specific problem

CampusHub aims to solve these problems by providing important college information in a simple, organized, and user-friendly way.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Students, especially new students, often waste time searching for information or visiting different offices to ask basic questions. Important information may be scattered across notice boards, websites, departments, and different offices.

CampusHub provides a centralized platform where students can quickly find college services, office details, document requirements, and campus information.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

CampusHub acts as a digital college guide. It provides important information through a simple dashboard and helps students access college services more easily.

The platform can include:

* Student login
* College dashboard
* Scholarship information
* Examination information
* Campus navigation
* Library details
* Department information
* HOD and office details
* Required documents
* Student support services
* AI chatbot for student questions

## 🚀 How to Run the Project

1. Download or clone the repository.
2. Open the project folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open the `index.html` file.
4. Right-click on the file.
5. Select **Open with Live Server**.
6. The CampusHub website will open in your browser.
